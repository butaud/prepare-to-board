# Jobs to Be Done

User stories for the three primary personas of Prepare to Board, organized by
persona and by when they touch the app relative to a meeting. This is a
planning document to guide gap analysis against the current implementation —
it intentionally includes jobs that may already be covered, so it stands as a
complete map of the job rather than a backlog.

## 1. Board Chairman — sets agenda, runs the room

**Between meetings**

- Create a new meeting and set its date.
- Build the planned agenda: add, remove, reorder topics.
- Set an estimated duration per topic and see total planned time vs.
  available meeting time, so I can tell if the agenda is overloaded.
- Add an outcome/goal to a topic (what decision needs to be made), so it's
  clear why it's on the agenda.
- Review open action items across the board (who owes what) and their
  due/overdue status.
- Nudge a member whose action item is overdue or due before the next
  meeting.
- Pull forward unresolved/deferred topics from the last meeting into the new
  agenda.
- Publish the agenda when it's ready, making it visible to the board.
- Edit a published agenda if plans change, with the change visible to
  members who already reviewed it.
- Skim the previous meeting's minutes before they're finalized to confirm
  accuracy.

**Immediately before the meeting**

- Do a last-pass reorder/trim of the agenda.
- Start the meeting, converting the planned agenda into the live agenda.

**During the meeting**

- See the live agenda with time elapsed/remaining per topic and overall, to
  know if we're on pace.
- Advance to / highlight the current topic for the room.
- Reorder remaining (not-yet-covered) topics on the fly.
- Cut/cancel a topic that won't fit.
- Defer a topic to a future meeting instead of cutting it.
- See at a glance which topics are done, current, upcoming, cancelled, or
  deferred.
- Insert an unplanned topic mid-meeting.
- Glance at the secretary's in-progress notes/motions for a topic to confirm
  accuracy without taking over editing.
- See a motion currently on the floor (mover/seconder/vote tally) to know
  when it's ready to call.
- End the meeting, marking it complete.

**After the meeting**

- Review the finalized minutes before they go out.
- Confirm every action item raised during the meeting has an assignee.
- See a clean list of new action items to track into the next cycle.

## 2. Board Secretary — minutes + housekeeping

**Between meetings**

- Maintain the board member roster (add/remove/edit contact info, titles).
- Manage organization membership and roles (invite members, assign
  admin/writer/reader).
- Distribute the published agenda/materials to the board.
- Finalize and distribute the previous meeting's minutes once approved.
- Clean up stale or misassigned action items.

**Before the meeting**

- Review the planned agenda topic order to prepare a note-taking structure.
- Confirm date/logistics are correct.

**During the meeting**

- Add free-text notes under whichever topic they're currently transcribing.
- Record a motion (text, mover, seconder, vote counts) and update its status
  as it moves through proposed → under discussion → passed/failed/tabled.
- Record an action item on the fly and assign it to a member from the
  roster.
- Highlight a topic for the room independent of which topic they're
  currently taking notes for — i.e., decouple "what I'm editing" from "what
  everyone sees highlighted."
- Jump back to an earlier topic to fix a note/motion/vote count without
  losing their current place.
- Add a late note to an earlier topic if something was missed, then return
  to the live topic.
- See which topics still have no notes, as a checklist to make sure nothing
  is skipped.
- Mark a topic's minutes as done/complete.

**After the meeting**

- Proofread/clean up minute text before publishing.
- Confirm no motion is left in an unresolved status.
- Confirm every mentioned action item has an assignee.
- Mark the meeting/minutes as finalized.
- Share/export the finished minutes.

## 3. Board Member — prepares, follows along, closes the loop

**Between meetings**

- See a personal dashboard of my open action items across all meetings.
- Mark an action item complete with one obvious action.
- Get a clear reminder for action items that are due soon or overdue.
- See the next meeting's date on a calendar view.
- Review the published agenda ahead of the meeting to know what's coming and
  what's expected of me.
- See the outcome/goal on a topic to understand what decision is being
  asked for.
- Read the previous meeting's minutes to refresh on what was decided.

**Immediately before the meeting**

- See the final agenda order/timing to estimate when my topic comes up.
- Notice if the agenda changed since I last checked it.

**During the meeting**

- See which topic is currently highlighted/live.
- See what's coming up next.
- See elapsed/remaining time to gauge pace.
- Read notes and motions as they're recorded, read-only, for transparency.
- See a motion currently on the floor and its vote status.
- Take private notes linked to a topic in the meeting, visible only to me.

**After the meeting**

- Get notified when minutes are published.
- See new action items assigned to me from that meeting and mark them done
  when complete.
- Browse past meetings/minutes, including my private notes.

## Cross-cutting jobs

Jobs that don't belong to a single persona:

- Switch between organizations (an account can belong to more than one
  organization).
- Manage personal profile/account settings.
